# Listening — 20 × 1-Minute Scripts (B2, ~140 words each)

TTS note: strip `N: / Woman: / Man:` labels before synthesis. Target ~55–65 sec at normal rate.
Pairs with `scripts/generate-listening-audio.mjs` (deepgram/sapi backends).

---

## 01 — Gym Membership Freeze

N: Listen to a conversation at a gym reception desk.
Woman: Hi, I'd like to freeze my membership for two months. I'm traveling for work.
Man: Sure, I can do that. There's a small freeze fee of ten dollars per month. Your regular rate stays the same when you return.
Woman: That's fine. Do I need to fill out a form?
Man: Just sign here and show your ID. Also, your locker rental will pause automatically, so you won't be charged for that.
Woman: Great. Will my trainer sessions carry over?
Man: Yes, the three sessions you have left will wait for you. Just book again when you're back in town.
Woman: Perfect, thank you.
N: Now answer the questions.
