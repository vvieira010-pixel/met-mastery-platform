# MET Speaking Practice — Quiz

Michigan English Test (MET) speaking prompts. Each item is an open-ended speaking task.
Set the question type to **Open-ended / Subjective** on Wayground and use the timer as the speaking limit.

| # | Prompt | Type | Time |
|---|--------|------|------|
| 1 | Describe the picture. Talk about the people, the place, and what they are doing. | Open-ended (speaking) | 60s |
| 2 | Tell me about a time when you worked on a project with other people. What was the project, and how did you contribute? | Open-ended (speaking) | 60s |
| 3 | Some people prefer studying alone, while others prefer studying with a group. Which do you prefer? Give your opinion and reasons to support it. | Open-ended (speaking) | 60s |
| 4 | Your friend has been offered a job in another city, but they are unsure whether to accept it because they would have to leave their family and friends. What are the advantages and disadvantages of accepting the job? | Open-ended (speaking) | 90s |
| 5 | A local community center plans to replace its quiet study area with a larger coffee shop. Some residents support the idea, but others disagree. You are the manager of the community center. Tell me what you think and try to convince me to agree with you. | Open-ended (speaking) | 90s |

## Scoring guidance (per MET speaking criteria)
- **Task fulfillment**: answers all parts of the prompt; stays on topic.
- **Fluency**: smooth delivery, appropriate pace, minimal hesitation.
- **Grammar & vocabulary**: range and accuracy suited to the task.
- **Pronunciation**: intelligible; stress/intonation support meaning.
