About MET Mastery

A focused learning workspace for people preparing for the Michigan English Test.

MET Mastery is built for nurses and other healthcare professionals who need practical English preparation connected to a clear goal. It brings together a starting diagnostic, targeted MET-style practice, teacher feedback, and progress review so a learner can see what to do next instead of receiving a disconnected list of exercises.

The learning model is evidence-led. A learner can practise speaking, writing, reading, listening, grammar, vocabulary, and test strategy. Work can be reviewed by a teacher, with explanations of what is working, what needs attention, and one practical next step. The platform is designed to support learning decisions; it does not replace an official Michigan English Test score or an institution's admissions decision.

Public scope: The agent-facing resources describe the product and its public capabilities only. Student records, teacher notes, submissions, and account actions are not exposed through the public API or MCP tools.
