MET Mastery

Privacy and data boundaries

Clear boundaries for learners, teachers, and public integrations.

MET Mastery is a learning platform. The application may process information needed to provide accounts, learning activities, submissions, teacher feedback, progress views, and related support. Access to learner and teacher data is controlled by the application and is separate from the public agent resources described on this page.

The public endpoints at /api/health and /api/v1/info are read-only and return service or product information. The public MCP endpoint provides the same kind of product and developer guidance. It does not expose student records, teacher notes, submissions, credentials, private prompts, or account-management actions.

Do not put personal information, passwords, private assessment responses, or access tokens into public URLs or agent prompts. If you have a privacy question or need help with a data request, contact vvieira010@gmail.com. This page is a public product statement and should be supplemented by any formal terms or notices that apply to your particular account or jurisdiction.
