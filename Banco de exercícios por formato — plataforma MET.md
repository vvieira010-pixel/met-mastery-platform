# Banco de exercícios por formato — plataforma MET

## Nota de uso

As atividades abaixo são **práticas originais no estilo MET — criadas para aprendizagem; não são itens oficiais do MET**. Elas foram organizadas por formato de exercício, e não como um simulado completo. As tarefas de *Listening* incluem um roteiro de áudio; na plataforma, o roteiro deve ser gravado e ocultado até a primeira tentativa. A correção indicada é formativa e não representa uma pontuação oficial.

---

## 1. Complete as lacunas (*Fill in the blanks / Cloze*)

**Instrução:** Choose the best option to complete each sentence.

1. The city library will remain open late ______ students can finish their projects.
   - a) although
   - b) so that
   - c) unless
   - d) whereas

2. Maya has worked at the community center ______ three years.
   - a) since
   - b) during
   - c) for
   - d) from

3. If the weather improves, the volunteers ______ the outdoor shelves tomorrow.
   - a) painted
   - b) will paint
   - c) have painted
   - d) would painting

**Gabarito:** 1-b, 2-c, 3-b. O feedback deve explicar a relação de finalidade, o uso de *for* com duração e o primeiro condicional.

## 2. Associe as colunas (*Matching*)

**Instrução:** Match each expression in Column A with its meaning in Column B.

| Column A | Column B |
|---|---|
| 1. reduce waste | a. a place where people borrow books |
| 2. reliable source | b. use less material that is thrown away |
| 3. public library | c. information that can be trusted |
| 4. take part in | d. participate in an activity |

**Gabarito:** 1-b, 2-c, 3-a, 4-d.

## 3. Ordene a frase (*Sentence ordering*)

**Instrução:** Put the words in the correct order.

> because / the meeting / was postponed / several members / were sick

**Resposta esperada:** *The meeting was postponed because several members were sick.*

**Critério:** A frase deve conter a oração principal antes ou depois da oração com *because*, mantendo a ordem interna correta e a pontuação adequada.

## 4. Ordene os eventos (*Sequencing*)

**Instrução:** Read the short account and number the events from 1 to 4.

> On Saturday morning, Leo collected the donated books. After lunch, he sorted them by subject. He then placed labels on the shelves. Finally, he invited neighborhood children to choose a book.

- ___ Leo invited children to choose a book.
- ___ Leo collected the donated books.
- ___ Leo placed labels on the shelves.
- ___ Leo sorted the books by subject.

**Gabarito:** 4, 1, 3, 2.

## 5. Escolha múltipla (*Multiple choice*)

**Instrução:** Read the notice and choose the best answer.

> **Notice:** The Riverside Community Garden will be closed on Tuesday morning while workers repair the water system. Visitors may enter after 2:00 p.m. Garden workshops will continue as scheduled on Wednesday.

Why will the garden be closed on Tuesday morning?

- a) A workshop will take place there.
- b) Workers will repair the water system.
- c) Visitors requested new opening hours.
- d) The garden will move to another location.

**Gabarito:** b. A resposta é explicitamente indicada no aviso.

## 6. Selecione várias respostas (*Multiple selection*)

**Instrução:** Read the announcement. Select **all** the correct statements.

> The neighborhood association is organizing a bicycle-safety event this Sunday. Participants should bring a helmet and arrive at 9:30 a.m. The association will provide bicycles for children who do not have one. Adults may attend the repair workshop after the safety demonstration.

- [ ] The event will happen on Sunday.
- [ ] Everyone must bring a bicycle.
- [ ] Participants should bring a helmet.
- [ ] Some children can borrow a bicycle.
- [ ] The repair workshop is only for children.

**Gabarito:** The first, third and fourth statements.

## 7. Verdadeiro ou falso (*True or false*)

**Instrução:** Read the text and decide whether each statement is true or false.

> The town opened a free study room inside the train station. It has desks, wireless internet, and charging points. The room is available from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., but users must keep conversations quiet because passengers use the space to work.

| Statement | True / False |
|---|---|
| The study room is located in a train station. |  |
| Users must pay to use the internet. |  |
| The room is open for sixteen hours each day. |  |
| Loud conversations are encouraged. |  |

**Gabarito:** True, False, True, False.

## 8. Destaque a evidência (*Highlight the evidence*)

**Instrução:** Read the paragraph and highlight the sentence that proves the answer.

> Many residents initially opposed the new bus lane because they feared it would increase traffic. After the lane opened, buses became more punctual, and local shop owners reported that more customers arrived by public transportation. The city is now considering adding a second bus lane.

**Pergunta:** Why is the city considering adding another bus lane?

**Resposta:** Because buses became more punctual and local shop owners reported more customers arriving by public transportation.

**Evidência esperada:** *After the lane opened, buses became more punctual, and local shop owners reported that more customers arrived by public transportation.*

## 9. Encontre o erro (*Error identification*)

**Instrução:** Identify the underlined part that contains an error.

> The volunteers **was** surprised when the number of donations increased **during** the campaign, so they **decided** to extend the project **for another week**.

**Resposta:** *was*.

**Correção:** *The volunteers were surprised...* O sujeito plural exige *were*.

## 10. Corrija a frase (*Sentence correction*)

**Instrução:** Rewrite the sentence correctly.

> If I would have more free time, I would join the evening conversation club.

**Resposta esperada:** *If I had more free time, I would join the evening conversation club.*

**Critério:** Usar o segundo condicional: *If + past simple*, seguido de *would + base verb*.

## 11. Paráfrase (*Paraphrasing*)

**Instrução:** Rewrite the sentence without changing its meaning. Use the word in parentheses.

> The workshop was canceled because the instructor became ill. (**due**)

**Resposta possível:** *The workshop was canceled due to the instructor’s illness.*

**Critério:** Preservar causa e consequência; depois de *due to*, usar um substantivo ou expressão nominal.

## 12. Combine as sentenças (*Sentence combining*)

**Instrução:** Combine the two sentences using **which**.

> The school installed solar panels. The panels will reduce electricity costs.

**Resposta esperada:** *The school installed solar panels, which will reduce electricity costs.*

**Critério:** Usar corretamente a oração relativa e a vírgula para acrescentar informação sobre *solar panels*.

## 13. Expanda a frase (*Sentence expansion*)

**Instrução:** Expand the sentence by adding a time expression, a place and a reason.

> The students presented their project.

**Resposta possível:** *Last Friday, the students presented their recycling project at the community center because they wanted to encourage local families to reduce waste.*

**Critério:** A resposta deve acrescentar os três elementos pedidos e manter uma frase gramaticalmente clara.

## 14. Resuma o texto (*Summarization*)

**Instrução:** Read the text and write a summary of **25–35 words**.

> A small grocery store began accepting reusable containers for rice, beans, and cleaning products. At first, only a few customers participated. After the store offered a small discount, more shoppers changed their habits. The owner says the program has reduced packaging waste without significantly increasing prices.

**Resposta-modelo:** *A grocery store reduced packaging waste by selling selected products in reusable containers. A small discount encouraged more customers to participate without greatly raising prices.*

**Critérios:** Incluir a iniciativa, o incentivo e o resultado; evitar detalhes secundários e copiar frases inteiras.

## 15. Tome notas (*Note-taking*)

**Instrução:** Listen to the recording and write no more than six key notes. Do not write complete sentences unless necessary.

**Roteiro para gravação:**

> The Oak Street Clinic will change its appointment system next month. Patients will first complete a short online form. A nurse will review the form and call patients who need urgent attention. People without internet access can complete the form at the clinic. The new system is designed to reduce waiting times, but emergency patients should still call the clinic directly.

**Informações esperadas nas notas:**

| Elemento | Nota possível |
|---|---|
| Quando | next month |
| Primeiro passo | complete online form |
| Quem revisa | nurse |
| Sem internet | complete form at clinic |
| Objetivo | reduce waiting times |
| Emergências | call clinic directly |

## 16. Ditado (*Dictation*)

**Instrução:** Listen and write the sentence exactly as you hear it. Play the recording twice.

**Roteiro para gravação:**

> The museum will offer free admission to local residents on the first Saturday of every month.

**Resposta esperada:** *The museum will offer free admission to local residents on the first Saturday of every month.*

**Critério:** Pontuar separadamente palavras corretas, artigos, preposições, plural, maiúsculas e pontuação.

## 17. Transcrição parcial (*Partial transcription*)

**Instrução:** Listen and complete the missing words.

**Roteiro para gravação:**

> The new evening course begins next Monday and meets twice a week. Students should bring a notebook, but all reading materials will be available online.

**Atividade:**

> The new evening course begins next ______ and meets ______ a week. Students should bring a ______, but all reading materials will be available ______.

**Gabarito:** Monday; twice; notebook; online.

## 18. Preveja o conteúdo (*Prediction*)

**Instrução:** Before reading the full text, look at the title and predict two ideas you expect to find.

**Título:** *Why Some Offices Are Creating Quiet Hours*

**Depois, leia:**

> Several companies have introduced two quiet hours each morning. During this period, employees avoid internal meetings and turn off nonessential notifications. Managers say the policy helps workers concentrate on complex tasks, although teams still need to coordinate emergencies.

**Resposta esperada:** Qualquer previsão plausível relacionada a concentração, menos reuniões, notificações ou produtividade. A atividade avalia estratégia de leitura, não uma única resposta correta.

## 19. Identifique a ideia principal (*Main idea*)

**Instrução:** Choose the best main idea for the paragraph.

> A university created a lending program for household tools. Students and residents can borrow drills, ladders, and sewing machines for up to one week. The program saves users money and prevents people from buying equipment they will rarely use.

- a) Universities should sell household tools.
- b) A lending program makes occasional tool use more affordable and sustainable.
- c) Sewing machines are more useful than drills.
- d) Residents must return all tools on the same day.

**Gabarito:** b.

## 20. Caça aos detalhes (*Detail hunt*)

**Instrução:** Scan the notice and answer using short phrases.

> The language exchange takes place every Thursday in Room 204 from 6:30 to 8:00 p.m. New participants should register at the front desk. The first session is free, but later sessions cost five dollars. Participants may bring a friend if they notify the organizer in advance.

1. What day is the exchange held?
2. Where does it take place?
3. How much does the first session cost?
4. What must participants do before bringing a friend?

**Gabarito:** 1. Thursday. 2. Room 204. 3. It is free. 4. Notify the organizer in advance.

## 21. Inferência (*Inference*)

**Instrução:** Read the dialogue and choose what can be inferred.

> **A:** Are you coming to the park cleanup on Saturday?
>
> **B:** I wish I could, but I have to work until noon. Maybe I can join you afterward.
>
> **A:** That would be helpful. We are meeting near the north entrance.

What can be inferred about B?

- a) B does not support the cleanup.
- b) B may join the event after work.
- c) B will organize the entire event.
- d) B has already cleaned the north entrance.

**Gabarito:** b. A inferência se baseia em *Maybe I can join you afterward*.

## 22. Vocabulário em contexto (*Vocabulary in context*)

**Instrução:** Choose the meaning of **allocated** as it is used in the sentence.

> The city allocated additional funds to repair sidewalks near schools.

- a) borrowed temporarily
- b) officially set aside or assigned
- c) secretly removed
- d) divided among students for an exam

**Gabarito:** b.

## 23. Formação de palavras (*Word formation*)

**Instrução:** Complete each sentence with the correct form of the word in parentheses.

1. The campaign was successful because of its clear ______. (organize)
2. Residents reacted ______ to the proposed changes. (positive)
3. The report provides useful ______ about local transportation. (inform)

**Gabarito:** 1. organization. 2. positively. 3. information.

## 24. Colocações (*Collocation practice*)

**Instrução:** Choose the word that forms the most natural collocation.

1. ______ a decision: a) do, b) make, c) take out, d) build
2. ______ attention: a) pay, b) give up, c) make, d) draw out
3. ______ progress: a) do, b) take, c) make, d) set

**Gabarito:** 1-b, 2-a, 3-c.

## 25. Flashcards (*Flashcards*)

**Instrução:** Show the English word first. The learner must say or type the meaning, an example sentence and one related word before revealing the back of the card.

| Frente | Verso esperado |
|---|---|
| sustainable | capaz de continuar sem causar dano excessivo; *The city needs sustainable transportation.* |
| affordable | que tem preço acessível; *The course is affordable for most residents.* |
| reliable | confiável; *The bus service is reliable.* |
| encourage | incentivar; *The teacher encouraged students to speak.* |

**Critério:** Considerar a palavra correta, o sentido apropriado e uma frase gramaticalmente compreensível. Usar repetição espaçada para os cartões errados.

## 26. Resposta rápida (*Quick response*)

**Instrução:** Answer aloud in **15–30 seconds**. State your answer and one reason.

> What is one useful service a public library can offer besides lending books?

**Resposta-modelo:** *A public library can offer free internet access because many people need a quiet place to study or complete online applications.*

**Critério:** Resposta relevante, posição clara, pelo menos uma razão e linguagem compreensível. Não avaliar pronúncia apenas a partir de uma transcrição.

## 27. Fale por tempo determinado (*Timed speaking*)

**Instrução:** Speak for **60 seconds**. Describe a change that would improve your neighborhood. Include the current situation, your proposal and one expected benefit.

**Roteiro de planejamento:**

| 0–15 s | 15–40 s | 40–60 s |
|---|---|---|
| Apresente o problema. | Explique a proposta. | Dê o benefício e conclua. |

**Critério:** Completar os três componentes, manter uma sequência lógica e sustentar a fala pelo tempo proposto.

## 28. Reconte a história (*Retelling*)

**Instrução:** Read the story for 45 seconds. Hide it and retell it in your own words.

> Nina missed her bus on the way to an interview. Instead of returning home, she used a public bicycle and arrived ten minutes early. During the interview, the employer asked how she had solved the problem. Nina explained her decision calmly, and the employer praised her resourcefulness.

**Pontos essenciais:** perdeu o ônibus; usou uma bicicleta pública; chegou cedo; explicou a solução; recebeu uma reação positiva.

**Critério:** Preservar os eventos principais e a relação temporal, sem exigir reprodução literal.

## 29. Descrição progressiva de imagem (*Progressive picture description*)

**Instrução:** Imagine uma imagem de uma praça em um sábado de manhã: há uma feira, duas crianças perto de uma barraca de frutas, uma mulher lendo em um banco, um ciclista chegando e nuvens escuras ao fundo.

Complete as três etapas:

1. **Básica:** Name five visible objects or people.
2. **Detalhada:** Describe what at least three people are doing.
3. **Inferencial:** Explain what may happen next and why.

**Resposta possível:** *There is a market in a public square. Two children are looking at fruit, and a woman is reading on a bench. The cyclist may leave soon because dark clouds suggest rain.*

**Critério:** Avançar de identificação para descrição e inferência; aceitar respostas diferentes quando forem coerentes com a cena.

## 30. Resposta a uma situação (*Situational response*)

**Instrução:** You borrowed a book from a community center, but you will not be able to return it by the due date. Leave a polite voice message explaining the problem and asking for an extension.

**Resposta-modelo:** *Hello, this is Daniel Costa. I borrowed a book that is due tomorrow, but I need a few more days to finish it. Could I extend the loan until Friday? I’m sorry for the inconvenience. Thank you.*

**Critério:** Identificar-se, explicar a situação, fazer um pedido claro e usar tom educado.

## 31. Role-play (*Role-play*)

**Instrução:** Work with a partner or AI tutor. Student A is a resident; Student B is a community-center employee. The resident wants to register for an evening class, but the available time conflicts with work. Negotiate an alternative.

**Objetivos linguísticos:** perguntar sobre horários, explicar uma restrição, sugerir alternativa e confirmar a decisão.

**Expressões úteis:**

> *Is there another session available?*  
> *I’m afraid that time conflicts with my work schedule.*  
> *Would it be possible to join the Saturday group?*  
> *That works for me. Thank you.*

**Critério:** Manter pelo menos quatro turnos de fala e chegar a uma solução ou explicar claramente por que ela não foi possível.

## 32. Debate rápido (*Mini debate*)

**Instrução:** Discuss the statement for two minutes:

> **Every city should make public transportation free for all residents.**

A pessoa deve apresentar uma posição, fornecer dois argumentos, responder a uma objeção e concluir.

**Roteiro possível:**

> *I agree/disagree because...*  
> *Another important point is...*  
> *Some people may argue that..., but...*  
> *For these reasons, I believe...*

**Critério:** Avaliar desenvolvimento das ideias, resposta à posição contrária, uso de conectores e clareza da conclusão.

## 33. Escolha e justifique (*Choose and justify*)

**Instrução:** A community center has money for only one improvement. Choose one option and justify your decision with two reasons.

- a) More computers
- b) A larger study room
- c) Evening childcare

**Resposta-modelo:** *I would choose evening childcare because it would allow more parents to attend classes. It could also help families who work during the day.*

**Critério:** Escolher uma opção explicitamente e fornecer duas razões relevantes, não apenas repetir a alternativa.

## 34. Revisão por checklist (*Checklist review*)

**Instrução:** Review the paragraph below. Check each item and rewrite at least two parts.

> Last weekend I visit the science museum with my friends. The museum was interesting, because we learned about renewable energy we also watched a short film. I recommend visit it to everyone.

| Item | Sim / Não |
|---|---|
| The verb tense is consistent. |  |
| The sentence boundaries and punctuation are clear. |  |
| The connector is used correctly. |  |
| The recommendation uses the correct verb form. |  |

**Resposta revisada possível:** *Last weekend, I visited the science museum with my friends. The museum was interesting because we learned about renewable energy, and we also watched a short film. I recommend visiting it to everyone.*

## 35. Avaliação de resposta (*Response evaluation*)

**Instrução:** Read the prompt and the learner response. Evaluate it using the criteria below.

**Prompt:** *Describe one way to make your school or workplace more environmentally friendly. Explain why it would help.*

**Learner response:**

> *My workplace should use less paper. This is a good idea. Paper is bad. We can use computers. It helps the environment.*

| Critério | Avaliação sugerida |
|---|---|
| Cumprimento da tarefa | Parcial: apresenta uma ideia e uma justificativa simples. |
| Organização | Clara, mas pouco desenvolvida. |
| Gramática | Geralmente compreensível; algumas frases são muito básicas. |
| Vocabulário | Adequado, porém limitado. |
| Próxima melhoria | Explicar como reduzir o papel e substituir “Paper is bad” por uma razão mais precisa. |

**Tarefa de extensão:** Reescreva a resposta acrescentando um exemplo concreto.

## 36. Reescrita orientada (*Guided rewriting*)

**Instrução:** Improve the response by applying the three suggestions.

**Resposta inicial:**

> *I think schools should have more clubs. Clubs are good. Students learn things and meet people.*

**Sugestões:**

1. Add a specific example of a club.
2. Replace *good* with a more precise adjective.
3. Explain one benefit in more detail.

**Resposta-modelo:** *I think schools should offer more practical clubs, such as a debate or coding club. These activities are valuable because they help students develop communication and problem-solving skills while meeting classmates with similar interests.*

## 37. Jogo de velocidade (*Speed round*)

**Instrução:** Answer each question in **5–8 seconds**. The goal is quick, accurate production rather than long answers.

1. What do you usually do after work or school?
2. Name one healthy habit.
3. What is one thing people can recycle?
4. Where can someone borrow a book?
5. What is one advantage of studying with a partner?

**Respostas possíveis:** *I usually cook dinner.* / *I drink plenty of water.* / *People can recycle glass.* / *At a library.* / *A partner can provide feedback.*

**Critério:** Responder a todos os itens com frases ou expressões compreensíveis, sem penalizar respostas diferentes que sejam adequadas.

## 38. Desafio de precisão (*Accuracy challenge*)

**Instrução:** Rewrite the paragraph. Keep the meaning, but correct all grammar and word-choice errors.

> *Last year, my town build a new park near the river. Many people was worried because the construction make noise. Now, the park have trees, benches, and a small playground. Residents says it is a useful addition.*

**Resposta esperada:** *Last year, my town built a new park near the river. Many people were worried because the construction made noise. Now, the park has trees, benches, and a small playground. Residents say it is a useful addition.*

**Critério:** Corrigir *build/built*, *was/were*, *make/made*, *have/has* e *says/say*, além de preservar o sentido.

## 39. Desafio de fluência (*Fluency challenge*)

**Instrução:** Speak for **90 seconds** about this topic without reading a script:

> **Describe a change you made in your daily routine and explain whether it was successful.**

Use the structure below only as a planning aid:

- What was the old routine?
- What did you change?
- What was difficult?
- What was the result?
- Would you recommend the change?

**Critério:** Avaliar continuidade, desenvolvimento, organização e capacidade de manter a mensagem. Registrar pausas longas, repetições e autocorreções separadamente; não transformar esses dados em uma pontuação oficial do MET.

# Modelo de dados recomendado para a plataforma

Cada exercício pode ser armazenado com os seguintes campos:

| Campo | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| `title` | Vocabulary in Context — Public Transportation |
| `exercise_type` | multiple_choice |
| `skill` | reading |
| `level` | B1 |
| `instructions` | Choose the meaning of the word in context. |
| `content` | Texto, áudio, imagem ou prompt |
| `options` | Alternativas, quando houver |
| `answer_key` | Resposta correta ou critérios |
| `feedback` | Explicação apresentada após a tentativa |
| `time_limit` | 60 segundos |
| `attempt_mode` | practice, timed ou review |
| `original_content` | true |

## Critérios gerais de feedback

Para questões objetivas, mostrar a resposta correta, indicar a evidência no texto ou no áudio e explicar a regra ou estratégia envolvida. Para escrita, comentar separadamente cumprimento da tarefa, organização, controle gramatical, alcance e precisão lexical e clareza. Para fala, comentar desenvolvimento, coerência, adequação comunicativa e controle linguístico; quando houver somente transcrição, informar que pronúncia, ritmo e entrega não foram avaliados integralmente.
