# Michigan English Test–Style Vocabulary Practice

## 45 questions at B1, B2, and B2+ levels

This original practice set contains **15 B1, 15 B2, and 15 B2+ questions**. Choose the word or phrase that best completes each sentence. The questions are practice material and are not official Michigan English Assessment test items.

## Part 1: B1 Vocabulary

**1.** We need to ___ the meeting until next Monday because several people are away.

A. postpone  
B. repair  
C. collect  
D. remove

**2.** The hotel is ___ near the train station, so guests can walk there easily.

A. politely  
B. conveniently  
C. suddenly  
D. honestly

**3.** Maria felt ___ before her job interview, but she became more relaxed after speaking to the manager.

A. nervous  
B. ancient  
C. narrow  
D. silent

**4.** Please ___ the form carefully before you send it to the office.

A. fill in  
B. break down  
C. turn off  
D. look after

**5.** The restaurant was so ___ that we had to wait outside for a table.

A. crowded  
B. empty  
C. private  
D. ordinary

**6.** You should wear a jacket because the weather may ___ colder this evening.

A. become  
B. borrow  
C. invite  
D. discover

**7.** I cannot ___ to buy a new laptop this month, so I will continue using my old one.

A. afford  
B. attend  
C. avoid  
D. announce

**8.** The teacher asked us to ___ the main ideas in our own words.

A. summarize  
B. separate  
C. whisper  
D. decorate

**9.** After walking for three hours, the hikers were extremely ___.

A. exhausted  
B. curious  
C. generous  
D. accurate

**10.** If you do not know the meaning of a word, you can ___ it ___ in a dictionary.

A. look / up  
B. take / off  
C. put / away  
D. give / back

**11.** The instructions were very ___, so everyone understood what to do.

A. clear  
B. bitter  
C. distant  
D. formal

**12.** We missed the bus, but fortunately a taxi driver agreed to ___ us to the airport.

A. drive  
B. grow  
C. lend  
D. melt

**13.** The company plans to ___ several new employees next year.

A. hire  
B. hide  
C. harm  
D. hurry

**14.** Daniel made a ___ decision to study abroad after thinking about it for several months.

A. thoughtful  
B. broken  
C. noisy  
D. lucky

**15.** The museum is free, but visitors are asked to make a small ___ if they can.

A. donation  
B. direction  
C. solution  
D. celebration

## Part 2: B2 Vocabulary

**16.** The new policy is intended to ___ unnecessary spending without reducing the quality of public services.

A. curb  
B. praise  
C. inherit  
D. overlook

**17.** Although the evidence was limited, the researchers drew a ___ conclusion from the results.

A. tentative  
B. edible  
C. vacant  
D. hostile

**18.** The manager asked the team to ___ the problem before deciding how to solve it.

A. identify  
B. imitate  
C. interrupt  
D. install

**19.** The documentary provides a ___ account of how the city recovered after the flood.

A. compelling  
B. reluctant  
C. accidental  
D. flexible

**20.** We need to ___ the financial figures before presenting the report to the board.

A. verify  
B. tolerate  
C. resemble  
D. consume

**21.** The professor encouraged students to ___ their opinions with specific examples.

A. substantiate  
B. postpone  
C. simplify  
D. distribute

**22.** The company has taken measures to ___ the impact of rising energy costs.

A. mitigate  
B. magnify  
C. memorize  
D. manufacture

**23.** The two proposals are broadly similar, but there is one ___ difference in their costs.

A. significant  
B. temporary  
C. residential  
D. accidental

**24.** Her explanation was so ___ that even a complicated technical process seemed easy to understand.

A. coherent  
B. cautious  
C. prosperous  
D. irrelevant

**25.** The witness gave a ___ description of the person she had seen near the building.

A. detailed  
B. flexible  
C. profitable  
D. artificial

**26.** The committee decided to ___ the proposal because it did not address the main safety concerns.

A. reject  
B. retain  
C. reveal  
D. restore

**27.** Many consumers are becoming more ___ of companies that collect personal data.

A. wary  
B. fond  
C. capable  
D. awareless

**28.** The agreement will ___ both sides to share information about future projects.

A. oblige  
B. persuade

D. release

**29.** The article examines the ___ relationship between sleep and academic performance.

A. complex

D. casual

A. crucial

## Part 3: B2+ Vocabulary

**31.** The minister’s statement was deliberately ___, allowing her to avoid committing herself to a specific course of action.

A. equivocal  
B. unanimous  
C. meticulous  
D. tangible

**32.** The report does not ___ the claim that the project created more jobs than expected; the data actually suggest the opposite.

A. corroborate  
B. allocate  
C. commemorate  
D. deteriorate

**33.** The researcher warned that the apparent improvement might be ___, since the study lasted only two weeks.

A. ephemeral  
B. impartial  
C. inevitable  
D. comprehensive

**34.** The company’s attempt to ___ criticism by publishing selected statistics failed to convince the public.

A. deflect  
B. derive  
C. decipher  
D. enforce

**35.** The agreement contains a ___ clause that permits either party to end the contract with thirty days’ notice.

A. provision  
B. deviation  
C. prejudice  
D. deficiency

**36.** The author’s argument is persuasive, but it rests on a rather ___ assumption about how people respond to risk.

A. tenuous  
B. lucrative  
C. impartial  
D. coherent

**37.** The committee sought to ___ the conflict by bringing the two departments together for private negotiations.

A. de-escalate  
B. anticipate  
C. proliferate  
D. authenticate

**38.** The evidence is not sufficient to ___ a direct causal link between the two events.

A. establish  
B. exaggerate  
C. relinquish  
D. fluctuate

**39.** The policy was criticized for being ___: it appeared to benefit large corporations while placing additional burdens on small businesses.

A. inequitable  
B. innovative  
C. resilient  
D. exemplary

**40.** The discovery has the potential to ___ our understanding of how the disease develops.

A. transform  
B. withhold  
C. confine  
D. undermine

**41.** Rather than solving the problem, the new regulations may ___ it by creating additional administrative work.

A. exacerbate  
B. alleviate  
C. authenticate  
D. consolidate

**42.** The historian attempted to ___ the conflicting accounts by comparing them with contemporary records.

A. reconcile  
B. provoke  
C. suspend  
D. distort

**43.** The proposal was rejected because its projected benefits were largely ___ and could not be measured reliably.

A. speculative  
B. compulsory  
C. transparent  
D. consistent

**44.** The organization has introduced strict procedures to ___ the misuse of confidential information.

A. forestall  
B. facilitate  
C. inherit  
D. commemorate

**45.** The speaker’s conclusion was a logical ___ from the evidence presented earlier in the lecture.

A. inference  
B. incentive  
C. impairment  
D. intervention
