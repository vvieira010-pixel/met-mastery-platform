# Additional Michigan English Test–Style Vocabulary Practice

## B2+ Vocabulary: Questions 46–55

Choose the word or phrase that best completes each sentence. These are original practice items and are not official Michigan English Assessment questions.

**46.** The committee’s decision to cancel the project was based on a ___ assessment of its likely environmental consequences.

A. cursory  
B. arbitrary  
C. rigorous  
D. peripheral

**47.** The new evidence may ___ the defendant’s account of what happened that evening.

A. vindicate  
B. impede  
C. provoke  
D. supersede

**48.** The policy was introduced to ___ the effects of inequality by providing additional support to disadvantaged communities.

A. perpetuate  
B. ameliorate  
C. preclude  
D. distort

**49.** Because the instructions were highly ___, different departments interpreted them in entirely different ways.

A. explicit  
B. impartial  
C. comprehensive  
D. ambiguous

**50.** The scientist remained ___ about the results, noting that the sample size was too small to support a definitive conclusion.

A. skeptical  
B. complacent  
C. receptive  
D. extravagant

**51.** The agreement was designed to ___ cooperation between countries that had previously competed for the same resources.

A. undermine  
B. foster  
C. constrain  
D. negate

**52.** The journalist was criticized for presenting a ___ interpretation of the data that ignored several inconvenient facts.

A. nuanced  
B. objective  
C. selective  
D. exhaustive

**53.** The organization must ___ its limited funds carefully if it is to complete all of the planned projects.

A. dissipate  
B. allocate  
C. revoke  
D. withhold

**54.** The witness’s testimony was considered ___ because it contradicted several independently verified facts.

A. credible  
B. consistent  
C. dubious  
D. conclusive

**55.** The company’s decision to publish the full report was intended to ___ concerns about a lack of transparency.

A. allay  
B. intensify  
C. precipitate  
D. circumvent

## Answer Key and Brief Explanations

| No. | Answer | Explanation |
|---:|:---:|---|
| 46 | C | **Rigorous** means thorough, careful, and demanding exact standards. |
| 47 | A | **Vindicate** means show that someone was right or not responsible for wrongdoing. |
| 48 | B | **Ameliorate** means make a difficult situation or problem better. |
| 49 | D | **Ambiguous** means open to more than one interpretation. |
| 50 | A | **Skeptical** means not easily convinced and requiring strong evidence. |
| 51 | B | **Foster** means encourage or promote the development of something. |
| 52 | C | **Selective** means involving deliberate choices about what to include or exclude. |
| 53 | B | **Allocate** means distribute resources for particular purposes. |
| 54 | C | **Dubious** means doubtful or unlikely to be reliable or true. |
| 55 | A | **Allay** means reduce or relieve fear, concern, or suspicion. |
